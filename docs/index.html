1

How it works

Duck Call listens for hits and moves your sound's level with each one. It sits in any FX slot on a synth, the drums or the master, and runs in stereo.

1.1Something hits

The red knob picks what makes the hits. The first eight are the beat grid, which follows the tape while it plays and keeps the OP-1 tempo when it doesn't, so playing keys pumps too.

The rest listen to audio: a tape track, all of tape, the input, or the sound itself. Each hit's loudness sets how deep and how long its dip is, like a drum struck harder.

1.2Duck, flat or gate

Flip, on white's page 2, sets what a hit does. Duck drops the level on each hit, the classic sidechain pump. Gate keeps the sound silent until a hit opens it. Flat in the middle does nothing, so sweeping through it fades one into the other.

Strength, on green, sets how deep. It gets deep from the middle of the knob up, and 100% goes to silence.

The bar is how far the level drops on a full hit. Measured on a steady tone in Duck Call's own code.

1.3Four shapes

White picks the curve the level follows after a hit, and the curtains draw it. Pump springs right back. Linear ramps. Hold stays down, then swells, the big breathing pump. Choke stays down almost to the end, then snaps open.

Attack, on blue's page 2, rounds every corner, from 0.2 ms to 100 ms.

The curtains · pump, linear, hold, choke

1.4Choke

Press red to switch choke on. A hit on the partner track then ends the current dip or gate on the spot. In gate it's the hi-hat trick: T2 opens the sound and a T1 hit slams it shut. In duck, a T1 hit lets the sound pop right back up.

The choke switch isn't saved with the sound, and the partner has to be a tape track that's playing back.

1.5From PercAll

Duck Call takes its ideas from Befaco's PercAll, a Eurorack module of decay envelopes, VCAs and choke switches: a trigger, a decay, a strength that deepens and lengthens each hit, and choke. Then it adds what the OP-1 makes possible.

2

The screen

An early 20th-century carnival duck shoot, drawn in the OP-1's own colors. Every part of the scene is tied to a knob, so you can read the whole effect at a glance. In silence it holds still.

2.1The ducks

Each hit knocks ducks down for as long as the dip lasts. The 1 of the bar takes the back pair, other hits take turns along the front row, and green ricochet dashes fly off each one. A down duck turns indigo and sinks.

Standing ducks wobble on every hit and duck their heads on a miss. The rifle fires a red flash on each hit, bigger on the 1, and the marquee bulbs chase along.

1/16 · full strength · choke shape

2.2The sights

The red sight changes with the source. Beat sources get a ring with a tick per pulse in the bar, and the tick that just fired lights white. Tracks get corner ticks for their number. Tape has spokes, the input is a peak, and SELF is a small mic.

Sights · 1/4 to SELF

2.3Waves and curtains

The blue waves are attack: sharp and choppy when it's short, rounded swells when it's long. They roll faster with a shorter length. The white curtains are the shape, and the rifle aims with flip: left for gate, center for flat, right for duck.

Attack · 0.2 ms and 100 ms

Controls

Four knobs, in the OP-1's colors. Push blue, green or white for a second page, and push red for choke. Everything but the choke switch is saved with the sound.

3.1Four knobs

The screen shows the real values, so green's label reads the actual depth, not the knob position. White and red snap to their steps.

A knob on its second page gets a ring inside it, and its value moves onto a chip of the knob's color.

Accent softens every hit but the 1: at 100% only the 1 is left. Sensitivity sets how loud a hit must be to count, and quieter ones become misses.

Install

Duck Call is one file. It needs an original OP-1 running hundekraft 3.2 or later, and installs through the hundekraft workshop in Chrome.

4.1Five steps

  1. Download duckcall.knl.
  2. Open the hundekraft workshop in Chrome.
  3. Go to Your plugins and choose Add your plugin files.
  4. Choose Connect my OP-1, then Review changes and Send.
  5. Press T1 on the OP-1 when it asks. Duck Call is now in every FX list.

To update, add the newer file the same way. Same name, so it replaces the old one, and your sounds keep their settings.

4.3Build it yourself

The source is on GitHub under GPL-3.0, the same terms as hundekraft. It builds with the hundekraft plugin SDK and a Blackfin GCC toolchain, and the screen art comes from a small generator script.

From hundekraft's ext/kennel
cp -r duck-call/plugin synth_sdk/plugins/duckcall python3 tools/kennel/pack.py \ synth_sdk/plugins/duckcall -o duckcall.knl

Play it

The same Duck Call logic, running in your browser with the OP-1 screen. Press play, pick a beat, and turn the knobs. The buttons stand in for pressing an encoder.

Blue: length
Green: strength
White: shape
Red: source
5.2 · Rhythm Machine · click a step to change the beat

The beat plays on the OP-1's four tape tracks: T1 kick, T2 open hats and cymbals, T3 snares and claps, T4 closed hats and percussion. Duck Call sits on the pad, and the drums stay dry. The red sight marks the track that triggers, the X the track that chokes. Each beat loads a matching recipe.
